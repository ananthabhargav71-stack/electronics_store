About Bhargav's Electronics Store

Bhargav's Electronics Store started as a single counter in Kurnool, repairing radios and selling spare parts to neighbours who trusted us to give it to them straight. That habit never left — we still test everything before it goes on the shelf, and we'll tell you honestly if a cheaper option suits you better.

Today we stock everything from phones and laptops to home appliances and gaming gear, but the way we work hasn't changed: real stock you can see in person, straightforward pricing, and a team that actually knows the products.

Every item that leaves our store comes with local warranty support — no shipping a faulty unit off to a call centre in another state.
